# Third-party software and assets

React and React DOM (MIT); sql.js (MIT), compiling SQLite (public domain); esbuild (MIT); Zod (MIT); @noble/hashes (MIT), used for portable SHA-256 review digests. Fonts: IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono (SIL Open Font License), Sora (SIL Open Font License), distributed through Fontsource. Exact installed versions and integrity hashes are in package-lock.json. Copies of the bundled packages’ LICENSE/OFL files are included in licenses/. Fonts and runtime libraries are bundled, not relabeled as original work.

No external images, music, third-party logos or stock video are part of the current interface. The Rowglass mark is original SVG/CSS geometry. Research papers are cited as sources; their code, datasets and figures are not copied into this implementation.
